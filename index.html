Your scans, on your device

Open a study

Choose the DICOM files from your scan disc or download, or open a ZIP archive. Select all files in a series together. A folder picker is available where your browser supports it.

Browse your images

Use Previous and Next above the scan, or move the image slider. The counter shows your position in the study, including time frames. In a multiple-view layout, choose which view to navigate. These controls work with any scan tool selected.

Use the viewer

Select a tool, then drag on the scan. Use Scroll for slices, Contrast for window/level, and Zoom / pan to move around. Two fingers zoom and pan. Study controls contain layers, colour maps, annotations, segmentation and export. Three-plane view requires a volume.

Install on your phone

On iPhone or iPad, open the Share menu in Safari, choose Add to Home Screen, then Open as Web App if offered. On Android, use Install app here or your browser’s Install app / Add to Home screen menu.

Work offline

Wait for “Available offline” before disconnecting. The app and image decoders are saved. Your scan files are not saved by the app; keep them on your device and reopen them each session. Remote study links require a connection. Browser storage can be cleared by your phone.

Updates

A new version takes effect after all app tabs and windows are closed and the app is reopened.

Report a load error

Expand Import diagnostics below the scan, then select Copy diagnostics. Review the report before sharing it. File names, scan contents and DICOM metadata are not included. The report stays in memory until you open new files or close the app.
